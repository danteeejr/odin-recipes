Description

This classic lasagna is made with layers of tender pasta, a rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta cheese, mozzarella, and Parmesan. Baked until hot and bubbly with a golden, cheesy topping, it creates a hearty, comforting meal that's perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 pound Italian sausage
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 (24-ounce) jars marinara sauce
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
